Excited to share my **E-commerce Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard** 📊🚀

In this project, I used **Excel** and **Power Query** for data cleaning, transformation, and merging datasets to build a complete and insightful dashboard.

🔹 **Data Preparation:**

* Cleaned and handled missing values using Excel
* Transformed and merged datasets using Power Query
* Prepared the data for accurate and efficient analysis

🔹 **Sales Insights:**

* 💰 Total Sales: **$27,027**
* 🛍️ Best-selling category: **Electronics ($13,651)**
* 📊 Category distribution: Electronics (51%), Home & Garden (35%), Clothing (14%)

🔹 **Customer Insights:**

* ⭐ Average Rating: **4.0** (High customer satisfaction)
* 💰 Average Customer Income: **$64,172**
* 👥 Average Age: **38**

🔹 **Revenue Analysis:**

* 📈 Top Age Group: **44–48 ($5,061)**
* Revenue is strongest among middle-aged customers

🔍 **Customer Feedback Analysis:**

* 👍 Most common positive review: *“The clothing arrived quickly and fits great!”*
* ⚠️ Most common complaint: *“Good product but could have been better”*

💡 **Recommendations:**

* Improve product quality and consistency to reduce recurring complaints
* Focus marketing campaigns on the 44–48 age segment (highest revenue group)
* Expand and promote Electronics products as the main revenue driver
* Maintain fast delivery performance as it’s a key driver of customer satisfaction

This project highlights how proper **data cleaning + transformation** can turn raw data into powerful business insights that support better decision-making.
